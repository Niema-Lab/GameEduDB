This course examines 2D (and 2.5D) visual design elements in games including sprite art and animations, backgrounds (static, scrolling, and parallaxed), particle effects, and UI/UX. The class will explore the fundamentals of graphic design (color theory, composition, hierarchy, typography, etc). Students will learn how to visually communicate concepts to an audience. Students will engage with ideation, communication, and problem solving for visual designs they learn to master the look and feel of a 2D game experience.
