Field Guide lays the groundwork for students’ academic and creative development by fostering self- directed learning, reflective practices, and foundational studio habits. The course supports students in setting meaningful goals and navigating college resources, skills that contribute to stronger engagement and preparedness in all future departmental coursework. While it does not directly alter other departments' curricula, it aims to enhance students’ readiness and agency as they progress into major-specific study. 3 credits.
