In this course, students engage in critical thinking, credible research, and writing skills in support of content production suited to a digital workplace. Beginning with the idea that field specific research is a necessary foundation for the creation of workplace media, this course provides students with skills necessary to move from knowledge gathering through to content production. The central goal is to demonstrate the role played by the research process in career-based writing for new media environments. Students will use their original research to support a written component, and a video or audio artifact intended for a public facing online platform. This course uses the writing process to support development of investigative skills and production of deliverables. Students will practice inquiry, active reading, research, written and oral communication, and documentation of sources.
