Prerequisite/Co-requisite: MAT 201 or MAT 203 or MAT 241 Much of our research in the communication field centers on understanding the audience. Who are they and how do we reach them? How do media messages affect individuals and society? During this course, you’ll learn the basics of conducting applied communication research, including why we do it and how research helps us, by collecting data and analyzing your findings in an original research project. Offered fall.
