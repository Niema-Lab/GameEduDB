The Internship in Game Development course is a supervised work experience in a game development setting. The specific work environment and student's job responsibilities must be approved, in advance. Students will be required to maintain a detailed journal relative to their workplace activities, establish specific learning goals, complete a reflective essay regarding the experience, and will be evaluated by their workplace supervisor. Students must work a minimum of forty-five (45) hours in the internship environment per credit hour earned. Students may receive from zero to twelve (0-12) credit hours for the Internship in Game Development course, which may be repeated up to two (2) times for credit; however, a student cannot earn more than a total of twelve (12) Internship in Game Development credit hours over the course of a single undergraduate program and only six (6),credit hours may be applied toward the minimum credit hours required for a single Coker University degree. Prerequisite: Completion of GAM 330 and completion of 54 semester hours.
