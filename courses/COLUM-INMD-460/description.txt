This intensive capstone course focuses on team leadership and producing a real-world project for an external client, with support from students enrolled in Design Team: Client Project I. As part of the leadership team, students will take charge of structuring the project, selecting methods, and overseeing the execution of the project while ensuring deadlines are met. This includes delivering multiple versions of the project throughout the process, refining it based on client feedback, and managing the overall workflow to ensure successful project delivery.
