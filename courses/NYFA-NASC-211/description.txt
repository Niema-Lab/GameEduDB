This course is designed as a survey of science across the physical and life sciences, including biology and biotechnology, environmental science, biodiversity and conservation, geology, astronomy, chemistry, geography, and climatology. Movies that demonstrate scientific concepts will serve as templates for elucidation of said concepts. Scenes will be examined, and the accuracy and portrayal of the science, and scientists, analyzed. Related topics, including the role of scientific advisors on films, and how best to balance “science” and “fiction” in film, are discussed. This course aims to raise an awareness of the treatment and content of science in popular films.
