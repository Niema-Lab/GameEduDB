Prerequisite: MAT 111, MAT 112, or MAT 201; or by permission of the mathematics faculty Students will be introduced to contemporary uses of mathematics, including problem- solving techniques and applications in sets and logic, right triangle trigonometry and vectors, and the mathematics of finance. Additional topics from linear systems and programming, matrices, probability and counting, voting and apportionment, and graph theory may be included as time allows. Offered fall and spring.
