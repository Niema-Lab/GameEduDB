Preparation for calculus and other mathematics courses. Exponentials, logarithms, trigonometry, polynomials, and rational functions. Satisfies no requirements other than contribution to the 180 units required for graduation.
MATH 1B may not be taken for credit if taken after MATH 2A.
Restrictions: Economics majors, Quantitative Economics majors, Business Economics majors, Pharmaceutical Sciences majors, Public Health Sciences majors, School of Physical Sciences students, School of Information and Computer Sciences students, School of Engineering students, Division of Undergraduate Education students, and School of Business students have the first consideration for enrollment.
