In this course, students will learn how to create a dynamic, professional, and interactive web-based portfolio that effectively showcases their projects. Through industry-focused research, students will develop strategic case studies that depict their creative process and approach to creative problem-solving to align with industry expectations. The course focuses on preparing students to present their work through practice in professional reviews, allowing the growth of professional networks and gathering feedback for future iterations. By the end of the course, students will be equipped with a polished portfolio and a deeper understanding of how to market their work to potential employers and clients.
