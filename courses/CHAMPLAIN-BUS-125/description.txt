In every career, success depends on how you work with people and within organizations. This course helps you do that well and with purpose. Grounded in the science of success, it introduces evidence-based practices in human-centered leadership, emotional intelligence, psychological capital, Appreciative Inquiry, and social responsibility. Through hands-on activities, you will enhance your collaboration, ethical awareness, and positive change skills while growing as an authentic leader ready to make a meaningful impact in any career or community.
