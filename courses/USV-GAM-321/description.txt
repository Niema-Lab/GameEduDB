This course covers the unique challenges and opportunities in designing engaging, user-friendly mobile games and crafting successful free-to-play models. You'll learn to optimize game mechanics for mobile platforms, create compelling user experiences, and implement effective monetization strategies. Emphasis will be placed on balancing gameplay enjoyment with monetization, understanding player behavior, and utilizing data analytics to refine and enhance game design. By the end of the course, you will have a comprehensive understanding of the mobile and free-to-play game landscape, equipped with the skills to create popular and profitable games.
