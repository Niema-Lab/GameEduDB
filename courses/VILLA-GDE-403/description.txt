The student, together with the rest of their cohort, will plan and design a significant, student-selected game project under faculty supervision. Students will be required to work together as a team to complete the pre-production phase of development. The Project Plan Documentation that must be completed includes a detailed schedule with Milestones and a full design document. The students will select and justify an appropriate implementation methodology, game engine or development environment, and create a vertical slice or playable demo for the project. A studio course. 3 credits
