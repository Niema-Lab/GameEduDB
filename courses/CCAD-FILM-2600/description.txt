This course introduces students to the principles and practices of sound design for moving-image media. Through hands-on projects, students explore sound recording, editing, mixing, and audio post-production workflows while developing critical listening skills and an understanding of sound’s role in storytelling. Emphasis is placed on production and post-production sound practices, including dialogue recording, sound effects, Foley, ambient sound, and mixing. Students utilize the Sound Studio and industry-standard audio tools to create and refine soundtracks for film, video, and other media projects.
