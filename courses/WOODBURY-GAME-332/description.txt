What this be the future of video games? An examination of the technologies of perception used to create immersive game experiences in the fields of virtual reality, mixed reality, augmented reality and alternate reality. We will explore devices designed to enhance users' perceptual experiences and the human sensory array that drives them. Students will design and implement immersive experiences for a range of technology platforms aimed at increasing the player's sensory experience. Studio. Prerequisite: GAME 221, Prototyping.
