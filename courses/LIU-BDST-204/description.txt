This course is designed to familiarize the student with digital audio production. Students will gain the ability to separate visual and sound information; develop a critical ear for recreated postproduction sound and cultivate a creative process for layering tracks in Adobe Audition. These topics have applications in the fields of radio, television, film, multimedia, broadcast journalism and web production, and prepares the student for advanced work in audio production. Students also examine how social media distribution shapes audio storytelling and develop platform-appropriate strategies for sharing and promoting audio work while applying professional and ethical standards. Students learn to use AI-assisted audio tools for tasks such as transcription, cleanup, sound design, and workflow support while evaluating outputs, disclosing their use, and retaining creative control. Pre requisites: BDST 211 and BDST 212
