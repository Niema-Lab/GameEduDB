The game industry is a big money business and spans the spectrum between the singular hobbyist and the massive-multinational corporation. This spectrum can make it challenging for VGDD students to visualize the entry footholds on such a broad career path. Students in this course will explore the obstacles and best practices that define a game developer from independent project studio to “Triple A” mainstream company. Students will be introduced to the functional, legal and financial aspects of a creative company at its various scales of operation. Students will model themselves as specialists and apply their skills to a variety of weekly experiments that build toward a final project.
