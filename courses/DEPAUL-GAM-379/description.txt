Students in this course iteratively develop compelling VR games / experiences by working independently or in cross-disciplinary teams. Students learn about the most exciting advances in the field of VR games and entertainment. There is still much to be invented in the kinds of play experiences VR makes possible by using the affordances of embodied input and immersion. Students are exposed to emerging technologies that transform and enhance virtual reality development such as generative artificial intelligence. Students are encouraged to explore experimental techniques, take bold risks, and determine their own vision of what VR experiences can be and what players do.
