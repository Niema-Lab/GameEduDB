Time-based visual media –– be they animation, photographic film, or games –– often require storyboards to align the teams creating them. This course delivers the art of pre-visualization, from abstract outline to detailed plan and pitch. Using both analog and digital tools, students will apply fundamental principles of draftsmanship and the virtual camera to prospective games and animation projects, which they will then pitch. - Prerequisite Definition: To take this course you must: Be enrolled in the following Plan GAA-BFA.
