Students develop mathematical modeling skills through application-driven projects in optimization, dynamical systems, probabilistic reasoning, and data fitting. The course emphasizes the application of the full modeling cycle to each problem, which includes problem identification, assumption-making, mathematical formulation, analysis, computational implementation, and critical interpretation of results. Students learn to translate real-world problems into mathematical frameworks and evaluate when models succeed or fail. Applications are drawn from games, data analysis, AI & machine learning, and other quantitative fields to align with student interests.
