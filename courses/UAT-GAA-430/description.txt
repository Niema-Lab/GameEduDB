Students will learn the importance of good planning and problem solving as they relate to character design for 3D animation. Course topics will cover: character setup, inverse kinematics, joints and bones, deformers, set-driven keys, bipedal and quadruped setups. Students will be immersed in hands-on motion capture exercises and explore advanced techniques for blending actions and mapping data to multiple characters. Students will create a fully functional character rig and apply animation to it.
