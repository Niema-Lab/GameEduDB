How does technological change impact people across the globe? What gets lost-or found-in translation? Through interdisciplinary analysis, this course examines technological change as a global phenomenon. We will examine how innovations from one perspective can be exploitative from another, investigating how technologies evolve and affect each other across different times and places. The course emphasizes ethical inquiry and considers how the economic, political, and cultural forces driving technological change affect people’s lives differently depending on context, identity, and access. Students will leave with a deeper understanding of how science and technology shape and are shaped by the world.
