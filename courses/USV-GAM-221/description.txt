Instructor Approvalor ENG101 This course provides an overview of Western-style ﬁction development as seen through the lens of story-driven video games. Starting with general theories of story such as the Monomyth and progressing to characterization tips and storytelling best practices, the course segues into an exploration of how these principles have been and can be applied by game developers to their own craft. Through a combination of lectures, readings, writing assignments, case studies, analytical exercises, and storytelling problem-solving, students will gain a better understanding of what it can take to bring a video game story to vibrant life.
