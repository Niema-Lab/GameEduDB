The communication practicum is a structured work experience that provides students with focused training in, and exposure to, an area or skill set used in communication and media fields. Unlike an internship, which often involves one student and one work site, the practicum may involve a team of students doing ongoing work to produce a deliverable, plan and execute a promotional campaign, generate content for a dynamic social media platform, or otherwise achieve measurable communication goals over time. The practicum must involve a public sharing of the deliverable(s). Both advisor and departmental permissions are required. May be repeated for a total of eight credit hours.
