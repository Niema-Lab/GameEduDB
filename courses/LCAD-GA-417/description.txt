This course builds on the foundations established in GA317 (Real-Time VFX) and dives deeper into the technical and artistic intricacies of VFX for professional game development. Building on core skills, students will deepen their understanding of timing, color, value, and visual impact to create effects that are not only functional, but cinematic and compositionally striking. Through hands-on projects and in-engine practice, students will explore complex material manipulation and refine their ability to craft visually satisfying effects—ranging from stylized to realistic. Using Niagara in Unreal Engine, students will gain greater control over particle behavior, explore dynamic simulations, and develop optimized workflows tailored for real-time execution. The course also introduces essential technical practices for managing VFX systems in a game pipeline, including performance considerations, modular design, and debugging. By balancing creative exploration with technical problem-solving, students will learn to think like both an artist and a technician—creating visual effects that feel alive, immersive, and polished.
