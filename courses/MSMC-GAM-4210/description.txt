This is the first course in a two part sequence in which students implement and verify an approved technical capstone game development project that they have researched and designed under the supervision of their principal faculty member. The student will begin the design, development and implementation of a game experience targeting at least one of the following: desktop systems; mobile devices; virtual reality; interactive video; interactive websites; social media; interactive art; interactive advertisement; interactive cinema. Completed projects will be fully documented and presented in writing, digitally and orally to appropriate audiences.
