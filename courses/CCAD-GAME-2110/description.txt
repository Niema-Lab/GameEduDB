This class explores the process of physical game production from idea to prototype. Students are required to present their final body of work at the end of the semester. Small teams are often required in supporting the diverse nature of game development. Each week presents core design topics in game building alongside studio time for faculty mentoring and peer-guidance. The evolution of each title will be thoroughly documented and presented for each major play-test session. Processes such as manufacturing components, digital marketing, crowdfunding, publishing, distribution channels, play-through demonstrations, and key game design topics are covered.
