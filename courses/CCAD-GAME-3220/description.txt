Level Design for Games introduces students to applied practice for building digital and physical 3D environments intended for player interaction, storytelling and immersive gameplay. Students will explore core topics in level design such as effective wayfinding, lighting, shape language, objective clarity, space purpose through layout sketches and modifying existing templates. Rapid iteration and documenting each modification are core exercises throughout class; moving from physical to digital. By the end of the semester students demonstrate the necessary skills in play-testing, building functional game levels, refining those assets that inhabit them and documenting play effectively. The use of assets produced in previous courses are encouraged.
