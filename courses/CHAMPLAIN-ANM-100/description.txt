This course explores animation as cultural knowledge across Western and Non-Western traditions. Students critically analyze diverse animated works, examining how animation knowledge is created and shared through films, techniques, and industry practices. Through inquiry-based learning, students investigate animation’s contribution to cultural conversations and artistic movements. The course examines technological innovation’s impact on production and distribution while applying critical frameworks to analyze representation, power structures, and global access in animation systems. This course fulfills the requirement for FRM-118 Knowledge Making in the Creative Arts, Media and Humanities.
