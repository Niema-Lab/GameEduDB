Making With Care explores what it means to utilize the elements and principles of 2D, 3D, and 4D design and the iterative process of making in a more intentional way. While process is still paramount in this course, additional attention is given to craft, execution, and conceptual approaches using a variety of media and methods. Course projects will require traditional and nontraditional studio materials, digital media, hybrid processes, and use of labs on campus. Students will engage their self-assessment skills through dialogue, writing, documentation of their work, and critiques.
