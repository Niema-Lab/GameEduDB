Game Jams are rapid iterative development sessions used to inspire creativity in the gaming industry sometimes conducted in as little as 24 hours. Although this course will not require the student to work quite as quickly, we will be completing roughly one game every week, sometimes every two weeks depending on the project. Through this rapid creation, limited and often simplified tools will draw out creative ideation and conceptualization. Every project will focus on a certain aspect or genre of game design. The aim in these methods is to allow a space for experimentation and to promote creativity, weirdness, and even failure. Through this process of prototyping, playtesting, and theory, students will learn and develop the core principles of game design and development.
