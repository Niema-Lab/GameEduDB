This course will cover the language of and the fundamentals of creating low polygon three-dimensional assets for video games. It will start with modeling basics: primitives and sub-object modeling using box, spline and polygon modeling techniques. The course will then progress to model unwrapping and mapping, creating custom textures for meshes and model optimization for export to 3D game engines. Students will also learn the fundamentals of rendering and animation in a 3D modeling program.
