This course provides students with a deep understanding of the game design industry. Over the course of the term, students will cover a wide range of business, management, and legal issues, including an overview of current business models in games and interactive media. Students will apply the video game development cycle encompassing the creation of a functional minimum viable product (MVP), rapid prototyping of game mechanics, and project management tools. Students will also learn methods for pitching and getting products funded. Prerequisite: GSD 210.
