Students will apply previously learned "game-ready" creation skills to create highly accurate 3D mechanical hero assets. Emphasis is on industry-standard workflows and best practices for asset creation, including precision modeling, optimization, baking, multiple tile UV sets, and texturing. Students will create an online presentation portfolio, which allows viewers to interactively manipulate and inspect their high-resolution 3D models. Prerequisite: GAME 237, Materials, Lighting and Modeling
