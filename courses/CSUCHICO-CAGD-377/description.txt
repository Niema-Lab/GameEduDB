This course investigates the evolution of mobile gaming and the challenges for developing mobile games, such as game design, art, programming, distribution, marketing, and monetization. The phases in mobile game development and various roles in a development project are explored. Existing and planned mobile games are analyzed. The process of planning, designing, implementing, and producing mobile games on various architectures is also investigated. Throughout the course, game concepts and proposals are created for analysis, with promising proposals moving on to planning, design, implementation, and development.
