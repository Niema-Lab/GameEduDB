This course focuses on developing the advanced writing skills required for contemporary business communication. The course explores various forms of professional writing, including reports, proposals, emails, memos, and digital content, with an emphasis on adapting to the rapidly changing communication landscape. Students will learn to craft clear, concise, and effective messages tailored to diverse audiences and purposes, whether in traditional business settings or modern digital platforms. The course also covers strategies for persuasive writing, structuring complex documents, and using appropriate tone and style in professional contexts. Special attention is given to the nuances of online communication, including writing for social media, blogs, and other digital formats. Through practical assignments and collaborative feedback, students will reﬁne their writing skills to meet the demands of contemporary business environments, ensuring they can communicate conﬁdently and effectively across various media.
