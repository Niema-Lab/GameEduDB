Students explore the complete lifecycle of professional game development from concept to prototype while learning the roles, responsibilities, and collaborative workﬂows of multidisciplinary game development teams. Through creative direction, world building, production planning, UI/UX design, and iterative prototyping, students develop an original game concept supported by professional pre- production documentation. Using industry-standard tools, students create a playable prototype and present a professional game pitch suitable for inclusion in their portfolio.
