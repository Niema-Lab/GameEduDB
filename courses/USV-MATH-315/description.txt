In this course student learn fundamental and applications of mathematical tools needed for graduate study in computer science ( and game design). Key concepts from calculus, probability, statistics, and graph theory are reviewed with an emphasis on application to real world problems. Topics include limits, inﬁnite sequences and series, derivatives and its applications, deﬁnite and indeﬁnite integrals, Applications of integration and simple differential equations, graphs and trees, Introduction to discrete random variables and probability distributions, analysis of algorithms.
