Advanced Game Development is a continuing study of the various aspects of the creative design of interactive games. This course is a combination of technology, design, and philosophy underlying contemporary game creation in the small, as well as the real-world implementation and considers the design challenges faced by practicing game designers. Topics may include how game designers create compelling content, designing for serious, persuasive, or educational games, and innovations in game mechanics and structure. Pre-requisite: GSD 210.
