This course teaches the writing of fiction. Students will be introduced to contemporary techniques, terminology, and revision processes that help writers create and judge their own material. Weekly submissions are required in order that students may gain confidence in their skills and help them polish their writing for the final portfolio. In addition, students will interpret diverse texts, which will inform their own creative work. Core outcomes met: O, Q, W. Prerequisite: Completion of university composition requirement will a grade of C minus (C-) or above.
