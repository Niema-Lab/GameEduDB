A study of the theory of creativity, the creative process, and factors that aid or hinder creativity, as well as the psychology of innovation, play, motivation, perception and expression. A study of theoretical positions in the psychology of creativity, art, games and play. The objective of the course is two-fold. The course will help students think critically about their own creative process; and how creativity, arts and play in a broad sense can be understood in a psychological framework. It will also explore key questions and theoretical positions in the psychology of games and play. Prerequisites: ENG100 College Writing I and ENG200 College Writing II (formerly HU100 Reading and Critical Thinking and HU105 Research and Rhetoric); grade of C- or higher required in each course Fulfills: Social Science Elective or Liberal Arts Elective or Art Education Psychology Requirement Elective (Art Education students) or Games, Toys and Play Requirement (GTP students); Art Education Minor elective; Art Therapy Minor elective
