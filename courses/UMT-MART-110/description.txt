This course explores the cultural, political, and social histories of games from antiquity to the present. Students will investigate how games function as primary sources and cultural artifacts across a range of historical and contemporary contexts, from ancient board games, sporting tournaments, and medieval carnival to modern video games and tabletop RPGs. By considering games as both texts and lived experiences, students will learn how playful pursuits have shaped culture, society, and the course of history itself. Through weekly case studies, hands-on playful experiences, and reflective writing, this course encourages critical inquiry and reflection on history through examining one of the most enduring and revealing forms of human expression: play.
