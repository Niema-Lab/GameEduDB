This course includes topics such as polynomials, radicals, exponents, coordinate geometry, introduction to trigonometry, rational expressions and equations, and solutions to linear and quadratic equations. Students are introduced to the concept of functions and their graphs. Additional topics may include matrices, linear regression, and linear programing. Emphasis will be placed on problem solving. A graphing calculator is required. Students cannot receive credit for MATH 1033 if they have credit for MATH 1034, MATH 1054, MATH 1063, MATH 1084, or any course for which MATH 1063 or MATH 1084 are prerequisites. A grade of C or better in MATH 1033 or MATH 1034 is required to take MATH 2043 - College Trigonometry.
