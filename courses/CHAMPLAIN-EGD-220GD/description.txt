Building on the foundational skills learned in CDS-180, you’ll analyze project challenges and create thoughtful solutions through iteration, while working collaboratively on a small team of students with different skill sets to create 2D game prototypes. You’ll evaluate ideas, integrate feedback, navigate diverse perspectives, and consider the ethical impacts of your work and the work of your team. You’ll adapt to changing project requirements, revise ideas, assess creative risks and apply appropriate project management approaches to your team’s work.
