Create highly detailed organic and hard surface 3D meshes using digital sculpting techniques. Then retopologize the high-resolution mesh, bake mesh maps, and create textures to ensure the finished asset is optimized for use in game engines.3D Modeling II builds on the subjects covered in 3D Modeling I with a focus on modeling organic forms using digital sculpting software and techniques. Although the course focuses on organic forms, digital sculpting technology and related workflows are prevalent in the modeling of solid surface objects as well. We will also focus on using 3D painting, mesh topology and hi-poly to low-poly baking.
