In a world shaped by generative AI, rapid innovation, and evolving workplace demands, the ability to think critically, adapt quickly, and leverage tools for strategic decision-making is essential. This course introduces students to modern management and entrepreneurial principles with an emphasis on real- world application. Students will explore core business areas—marketing, operations, HR, ﬁnance, and strategy—through dynamic simulations, AI tools, and team-based projects. This course prioritizes adaptability, ethical decision-making, and the capacity to learn and lead in complex, evolving business environments.
