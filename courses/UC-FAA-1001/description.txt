Foundation Level Studio course with a focus on the development of strong visual awareness that leads to sophisticated and informed mark-making in drawing. The course emphasizes the comprehension, creation, and analysis of observational and representational drawings demonstrating an understanding of one- and two-point perspective, atmospheric perspective, value, structure, texture, and craftsmanship using a variety of traditional media. The course will also focus on exploring innovative and experimental approaches to mark-making and composition and may include a range of digital media. The course includes in-class and homework drawings of varying duration, demonstrations, critiques, lectures, research, and the presentation of professional artists working in drawing.
