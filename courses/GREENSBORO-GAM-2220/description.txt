Students use a social lens to explore how games may promote or disrupt nuanced examinations of groups and identity. Do individuals behave differently when playing alone versus with others? How do players seem to change when they go online, such as in massively multiplayer games? How do games help us recognize and address social differences in race, class, gender, family, sexuality, nationality, or religion? How can we use games to build better and more inclusive communities, and what safeguards might be used in games to provide a healthy gaming environment?
