In this course, you’ll transform everyday challenges into creative solutions through hands-on projects. You’ll learn the basics of design thinking, problem-posing, and project management while working in teams. By methodically brainstorming ideas, developing prototypes, and presenting your work, you’ll build essential collaboration and communication skills. The course encourages you to embrace failure as a valuable part of the process, adapt to changing circumstances, and apply these fundamental practices in both workplace and community settings.
