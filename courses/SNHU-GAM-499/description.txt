In this course, students will develop and execute the skills needed to design, plan, propose, and implement technical solutions. They will explore project management techniques while planning the development of technical solutions, ensuring that these solutions meet the scope and timeline requirements. Students will utilize design frameworks along with problem-solving and research strategies to create a formal solution proposal. Documentation of the solution, along with the iterations and retrospective feedback, will be included as part of the solution implementation. Additionally, students will prepare for technical interviews, build professional networks, and develop a portfolio to support their career goals.
