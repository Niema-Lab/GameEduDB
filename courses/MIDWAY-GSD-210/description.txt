This class examines the fundamentals of designing and developing games. Students use creative and technical skills as they learn about the many aspects involved with designing video games. Students learn conceptual and practical skills for bringing a comprehensive artistic vision to the creation of games, including concept art, character design, level design, storytelling, and gameplay. Topics of study will also include the procedure of designing games including conceptualization, prototyping, and playtesting. Pre-requisite: GSD 200 Introduction to Game Studies.
