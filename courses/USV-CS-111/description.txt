In this course, students are introduced to the procedural computer programming paradigm, including a foundation in Boolean logic. Students learn practical hardware topics such as CPU, memory, disks, and ﬁles as well as lexical elements, operators, fundamental data types, ﬂow of controls, functions, recursions, arrays, pointers, strings, bit-wise operators, structures, unions, ﬁle manipulation. Standards of program development ﬂow and structured programming paradigm are also covered.
