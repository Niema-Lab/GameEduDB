The first semester of this class is an introduction to the fundamentals of visual storytelling, storyboarding, and film language as relevant to the fields of animation, film, and video games. Students will develop the skills and sensitivities essential to design and communication for the screen. In the following two semesters students will build upon these fundamentals and expand upon their knowledge of the techniques, tools, software, and language of story development, storyboarding, and cinematography.
