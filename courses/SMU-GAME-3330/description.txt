The art and technology of emerging communication in human virtual environment interaction (HVEI). Extended reality content designers explore how to tap into a form of haptic intelligence, placing technological sensory output interfaces where the body is kinesthetically linked to the felt body via proprioception human interfaces: visual, auditory, haptic, and locomotory displays; position tracking and mapping in order to immerse an audience in a continuously updated computer-generated synthetic environment. Prerequisite: DSIN 2350.
