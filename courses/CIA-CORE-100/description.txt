CORE 100 introduces students to a process-based approach to art and design grounded in inquiry, iteration, and exploration. Through topic-based studios, students investigate ideas across analog and digital methods, engaging a shared vocabulary of critical concepts and creative strategies. Emphasis is placed on understanding and applying different creative processes, alongside reflection and documentation, as students begin to connect thinking and making while developing universal skills for communicating creative outcomes. 3 credits. Repeatable for up to 6 credits.
