In this studio course, students will create original environments (often known as visualization or concept art) that can be applied to game and animation projects, and study the work of professional artists working in the field. Course activities will include lectures, demos, student presentations, studio work, and critiques. - Prerequisite Definition: To take this course you must: Have taken the following Courses FAA1001 min grade C-,FAA2021 min grade C-, or FAA1051 min grade C-.
