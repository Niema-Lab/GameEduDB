This course provides an overview of the field of sport management, offering a diversity of information about the history and foundations of the discipline in addition to concepts and practices of effective management at the professional, collegiate, and high school levels. Students will explore the various sport industry segments, international sport, and current ethical and social issues in sport. An immersive examination of management elements of sport, including but not limited to, sport finance, sport facilities, sport marketing, sport media and communications, and sport law are incorporated into the course. Offered fall.
