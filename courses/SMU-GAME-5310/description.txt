For game design majors in their final year of studies. A practical and informed approach to understanding the competencies that are required to sustain practice as an artist beyond the undergraduate experience. Students learn how to negotiate the professional aspects of art and to identify and take advantage of a host of opportunities. Topics include establishing a studio; applying for grants; exhibiting work; intellectual property law; the markets for videogames; and alternative models of production, distribution, and exchange of videogames. A submission of an artist statement is required.  Prerequisite: GAME 3390.
