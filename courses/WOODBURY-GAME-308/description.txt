Students will design, develop and produce an original three-dimensional animated short. Production practices will include concept art development, the creation of motion-based storyboards, and the use of industry-standard 3D and motion software. Students will learn to bring 3D objects to life with overlapping, compound motions expressing the influences of gravity, weight, and momentum. Traditional key-frame animation will be digitally integrated with motion-capture data to tell an expressive, original story. Prerequisite: GAME 307, Character Rigging.
