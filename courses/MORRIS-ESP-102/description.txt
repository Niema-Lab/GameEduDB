Performance and Performance Enhancement Tools in Esports introduces the students to the performance-related esports based on sports science principles. Team and individual esports skills, coaching and analysis and motor control are components of this course. The role of neurocognitive brain stimulators such as Transcranial Direct Current Stimulation (tDCS) and anodal tDCS (a-tDCS) as well as behavior modification and physical exercises and rest, is explored. Three (3) lecture hours per week
