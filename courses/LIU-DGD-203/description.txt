This course provides students to opportunity to read and write about games from an academic perspective. Students will read works ranging from Dutch historian Johan Huizinga's Homo Ludens, an early 20th-century sociological study of the role of play among humans, to Mary Flannagan's Critical Play, a much more modern look at games and their influence on modern art. While the core of the class is based on weekly readings and student responses, students will write major papers during the semester. Examples from both historical games and modern games will be used in class discussions. A pre requisite of DGD 202 is required.
