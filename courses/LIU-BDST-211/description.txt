This course introduces the student to the basics of digital audio production. Students are introduced to the principles of sound, analog and digital recording, and the basics of sound editing. These topics have application in all forms of journalism, broadcasting, film, and on the internet. Students also explore audio created for social media, including platform-appropriate formats, captioning, accessibility, and responsible publishing practices. Students are introduced to responsible uses of AI- assisted audio tools for transcription, editing, restoration, and ideation, with attention to accuracy, disclosure, copyright, and creative ownership.
