In this course, we will explore how design and visual communication shape knowledge across cultures and history. Moving beyond Western-centric narratives, we will study global perspectives-from ancient writing systems to digital media-while engaging ideas from feminism, postcolonial theory, semiotics, and critical race theory. Through hands-on projects, we will connect historical analysis with creative practice, building critical thinking skills and producing original work that shows how creative expression both reflects and challenges cultural understandings. This course fulfills the requirement for FRM-118 Knowledge Making in the Creative Arts, Media and Humanities.
