Students explore representations of sports organizations, sports celebrities, competitive events, and athletic controversies across different media. Different kinds of sports journalism—such as sports writing, interviewing, live broadcasting, and commentating—are compared and analyzed to determine effective tone, target audience, and style given the purpose and venue. The course may draw from selected films and fictional works in order to highlight challenges encountered when covering sports. Students are encouraged to explore issues that frequently make the news, including diversity efforts; gender parity; celebrity athletes; the role of sports in promoting social justice; prevention of cheating; use of performance-enhancing drugs; competing fitness regimens; and media representations of coaches and coaching.
