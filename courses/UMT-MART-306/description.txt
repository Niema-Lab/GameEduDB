With an emphasis on 3D character development, this course covers concepts for creation in digital 3D sculpting techniques and applications. This course works in conjunction with the 3D-modeling course and acts as a foundation for hard surface modeling and environments. This course teaches soft surface modeling and emphasizes character design through a specific 3D workflow known as sculpting. The goals of this course is to develop the skills and workflow in soft surface, from sculpting, to retopology, texture painting, rigging, animation, and integration with a game engine.
