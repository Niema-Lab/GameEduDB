This is an upper-level course in 3D modeling and animation using Autodesk Maya 3d software. Students build 3D models and render them as digital animations. Emphasis is placed on principles of digital 3D design for animation and illustration as well as 3D character development for motion pictures and digital game design. Game Design Students: a pre requisite of CGPH 216 is required. Digital Arts Students: a pre requisite of CGPH 207 and CGPH 212 is required.
