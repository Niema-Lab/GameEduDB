This course will immerse the students in the intricacies of body mechanics including: weight, balance, line of action, locomotion, inertia, and acceleration. Adding the complexities of a full body character, this course will examine interactions between characters and objects as well as other characters. Students will also study motion capture and how to integrate that in their work. In addition to the study of body mechanics, students in this course will assist capstone students in producing their final projects, emulating the professional studio environment. - Prerequisite Definition: To take this course you must: Have taken the following Course GAA1020 min grade C-. Be enrolled in the following Plan GAA-BFA.
