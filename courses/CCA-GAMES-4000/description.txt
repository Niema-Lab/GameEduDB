Senior Game Project 1 is the first of a two-semester sequence in which students conceive, develop, and produce their capstone Game Arts project. This original game might be for digital, analog, mixed, or an experimental game mediums, and it may be undertaken alone or in-collaboration with student peers. In Senior Game Project 1 students will explore and revise original game design concepts, develop aesthetic design of art, characters, mechancis and systems, gameplay and level design, while adhering to a production plan for their original game project. Working under faculty supervision, students will lead their own development process and create a Game Design Document, develop iterative Rapid Prototypes, playtest, and create a working demo version before continuing production into the following semester.
