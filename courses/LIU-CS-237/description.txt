This course discusses human factors concepts applied to human-computer interface design. Three generations of interface paradigms are examined in relation to the historical development of hardware and software. Students will explore various development methodologies that have evolved and learn how to organize and plan for usability testing. Students will gain an understanding of the importance of human perception in the development of digital interfaces and the types of strategies involved in that process. Design theories are applied to usability testing problems. Prerequisites: CS 206, CS 216, and CS 229
