Labs is a workshop-based course that invites students to investigate a wide range of materials, tools, and techniques across art and design practices. Through focused, hands-on workshops, students build skills while experimenting with new approaches that expand creative possibilities. Each workshop offers an immersive experience with a process, skill or material, allowing students to explore its creative potential and uses within their own practice. By selecting from a rotating series of short workshops, students exercise agency in shaping their learning experience, while reflecting on how each prcess contributes to their evolving creative identity. Emphasis is placed on working safely, responsibly, and with attention to craft within studio spaces across the College. 3 credits.
