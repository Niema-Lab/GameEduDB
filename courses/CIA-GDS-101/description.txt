This course introduces students to the principles and practices of graphic design through hands-on projects while grounding thinking and making in historical and contemporary contexts. Students will form a foundation of knowledge and ability in the fundamentals of visual communication such as typography, image- and form--making, composition, color, and the elements and principles of design, all while asking: for whom are these designs for and to what ends? With a focus on experimentation and reflection, students will foster curiosity and criticality through projects, lectures, workshops, and exercises, supported by presentations and critiques. To augment their studio-based experiences, students will explore questions and discuss insights through reading, watching, and listening to a range of relevant sources. 3 credits.
