An intensive study designed to further integrate computational media and digital processes into creative practice. This course explores the fundamentals of game design. Students explore game design by analyzing and deconstructing videogames, critiquing serious games, and discussing current trends. It provides a foundational framework and language for discussion. The focus is on developing independent work, group collaboration, and analytical study applied to game design. Prerequisite: 3 credit hours of ASIM, ASDR, ASSC, ARHS, FILM, CRCP, ADV coursework, or instructor approval.
