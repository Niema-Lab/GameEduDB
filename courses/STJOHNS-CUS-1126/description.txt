Prerequisite: CUS 1116. Introduction to data structures concepts, including lists, stacks, queues, and trees using Java. Students will learn how to implement algorithms to perform specific tasks such as sorting and searching, and will also explore the use of graphical user interfaces. A minimum grade of C is required in CUS 1116 and CUS 1126 courses, for students to be allowed to continue in the computer science major (including computer science option programs).
