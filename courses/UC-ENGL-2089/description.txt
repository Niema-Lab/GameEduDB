Intermediate Composition is a writing-centered course that builds on what students learn in first-year composition and focuses students’ attention on theoretical underpinning of how meaning is made, understood, and communicated within and across various discourse communities and genres. The course emphasizes critical reading and writing, advanced research and analysis skills, and rhetorical sensitivity to differences in academic, professional, and public composing. This course challenges students to engage in substantive projects drawing on primary research and source analysis methods and asks students to document, communicate, and reflect on their research. - Prerequisite Definition: To take this course you must: Have taken the following Courses ENGL1001 min grade C-, or ENGL1001C min grade C-, or ENGL1002 min grade C-, or ENGL1012 min grade C-, or ENGL1902 min grade C-. Be at least a Sophomore.
