A thematic investigation of art in diverse social, cultural, and historical contexts. This course explores how artists, art collectives, artworks, and institutional structures interact with their respective cultural moment(s) and are received by audiences and artists in the present. This course examines the ways in which artists, artist assistants, audiences, patrons, curators, galleries, public spaces, inventors, artists, and technologies shape artistic practices, and how these artistic practices in turn shape our identities.
