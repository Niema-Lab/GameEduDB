The Project and Portfolio I: 3-D Arts course combines hands-on learning experiences with summative and formative portfolio assessments. In this course, students will apply their artistic skills and technical knowledge to create hard-surface models and quality texture maps based on reference material they have compiled. This course provides students experience with production deadlines and constraints. Using reflection and independent study to reinforce discipline topics, students will work through practical exercises and refine their work for presentation in a student portfolio.
