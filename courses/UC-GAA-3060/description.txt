Working in a production studio or on any project involving other artists, requires a notion of how the workflow must perform in order to finalize the work. Students in this course will be providing storyboard, animation, asset creation, programming, systems testing, and other efforts to larger projects. Students will learn how to successfully collaborate, iterate, and coordinate creative efforts, as well as contribute to another potential project for their portfolios. Students will also refine their own skills in detail work and component design. - Prerequisite Definition: To take this course you must: Be enrolled in the following Plan GAA-BFA. Be at least a Junior.
