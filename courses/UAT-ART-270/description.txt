This course delves into the exciting realm of AI art, offering students a unique opportunity to fuse creativity with cutting-edge technology. Students will combine theoretical knowledge with hands-on experience to investigate how AI art can improve upon a wide variety of creative technology-based workflows, pipelines, and outputs. Additionally, students will research and discuss the ethics surrounding the use of AI artwork, its history, and its potential future. The class will not only give students a better understanding of AI art, but it will also allow them to produce artworks that demonstrate their creativity in the age of AI.
