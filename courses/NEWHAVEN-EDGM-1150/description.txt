This course explores how the esports industry started and has grown over the past 40 years. The class will explore whether esports is truly a sport, all the various stakeholders and their roles, the ethical issues associated with esports, the future of esports, and careers in esports. This is the introductory course designed to provide a general overview of the industry for both gamers and those unfamiliar with the industry. Students will plan and execute an esports tournament. 3 credits.
