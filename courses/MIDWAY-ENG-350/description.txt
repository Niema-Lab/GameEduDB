In this course, students will draw on a range of scholarly frameworks to analyze digital texts. Students will learn fundamental concepts in critical analysis, read scholarship about digital texts, and engage with digital texts to extend and challenge our notions of communication across media. Upon completion, students will be able to: Develop an awareness of the roles digital texts play in contemporary culture. Demonstrate an awareness of discipline-appropriate critical theory and understanding of how to apply these concepts to digital texts. Demonstrate an awareness of how digital texts communicate and articulate arguments about how digital texts work with and/or against concepts from critical theory. Demonstrate an awareness of how to use academic methods of research and critical theory to understand how it relates to digital texts. Demonstrate an awareness of digital texts, their history, their production aesthetics, and how the unique affordances of digital texts connect with critical theory. Develop a deeper understanding of digital texts by writing interpretive and critical analyses.
