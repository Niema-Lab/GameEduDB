This course exposes students to developing digital games using game engine(s). The course will introduce a set of tools, platforms, mods, and engines, which will change from year to year at the discretion of the instructor. Possible tools include Unity, Unreal, Construct 3, GameMaker, and Godot. Depending on the chosen tools, students will design two-dimensional games, advancing to the development of three-dimensional games if possible. No prior game engine experience is necessary.
