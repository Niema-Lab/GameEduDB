Offered in the fall semester, this is a first-year requirement focusing on the fundamentals of oral and written communication such as the planning, writing, and revising of a thesis-driven essay following guidelines and standards of the Modern Language Association (MLA), information literacy skills, and extemporaneous discussion and public speaking skills. Students will demonstrate college-level critical thinking and analysis skills through the close examination of and response to selected works of nonfiction, literature, and poetry.
