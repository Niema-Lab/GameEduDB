In this course, students will examine how historical forces have influenced the emergence of creative technologies and cultural production. Through the study of various artists, art works, and artistic movements, along with theories and philosophies of art, they will develop research questions, analyze, and apply critical frameworks to discussions of artistic practice. Students will consider how global power structures have affected and continue to affect the work of artists and scholars. Being the most theory-based course in the FRM block, ART 120 will bolster students’ analytical understanding of activities in the cultural sphere, advancing their critical capacities that complement technical skills, artistic practice, and creative development. This course fulfills the requirement for FRM-128 Global Contexts in the Creative Arts, Media and Humanities.
