Designed to help students navigate their academic journey while preparing for post-graduation career opportunities. Topics include campus resources, ways to get involved in the school community, and time-management strategies for completing assignments on schedule. Also explores strategies for networking and building a professional identity for transitioning from college to career. Required for all students in their first semester majoring in the Harry W. Bass Jr. School of Arts, Humanities, and Technology who were not required to take AHTC 1100. Credit cannot be received for both courses, AHTC 1100 and AHTC 2100.
