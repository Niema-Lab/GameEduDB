What does it take to succeed in the global game industry? This course examines the business strategies, market trends, and cultural forces shaping how games are developed, marketed, and distributed worldwide. Students explore the operational, financial, legal, and socio-political dimensions of international game business - from licensing and localization to esports and digital platforms - and apply strategic frameworks to real markets and case studies. Designed for students pursuing careers in publishing, marketing, distribution, live services, or development.
