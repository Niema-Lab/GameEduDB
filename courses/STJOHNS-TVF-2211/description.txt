Creates realistic 3D assets for high-end visual effects in film and TV commercials. Students will learn various professional approaches, such as NURBS (Non-uniform rational basis spline) and Polygon 3D modeling methods to create realistic 3D models. It will also cover lighting, texturing, essential camera movement, assets rendering, and positioning cameras in 3D scenes. Students will learn to embed professional 3D production pipelines and workflows concepts in film production.
