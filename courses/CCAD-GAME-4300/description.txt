Game Projects Capstone II provides students a supportive environment to finalize their capstone project for thesis study. Collaboration is strongly encouraged. Students will polish and test their original game experiences, and, have new opportunities to share their games with the local development community and campus play-testers. Conference and festival submissions are strongly encouraged. Students will be required to iterate on their design choices and visual content based upon player feedback and industry trends. Rigorous documentation and reflection is required among official online platforms such as dev logs and game-play videos. Students will be required to share their final experience in an official format including a multimedia pop-up exhibit and live streamed retrospective.
