3D Animation I: Principles introduces students to the fundamental concepts and techniques of 3D animation. This course covers the core principles of animation, including timing, spacing, weight, and movement, as well as the basics of 3D modeling, rigging, and keyframe animation. Through hands-on projects and exercises, students will learn how to bring characters and objects to life in a three- dimensional space, focusing on creating believable motion and expression. By the end of the course, students will have a strong foundation in the principles of 3D animation, preparing them for more advanced work in subsequent courses. This course is essential for anyone pursuing a career in digital art, animation, or related ﬁelds.
