Students examine how academic and professional expertise translates into public discourse, cultural products, and social interventions. Building on critical inquiry skills and community analysis frameworks, students investigate the complexities of public engagement through case studies, grant proposal development, and critical examination of public humanities projects. The course emphasizes process-oriented learning and methodological competency over product creation, using critical questioning frameworks to analyze power dynamics, institutional constraints, and questions of benefit in public work. Students develop capacities as emerging public intellectuals who can translate specialized knowledge while maintaining critical consciousness about extraction, representation, and public value. Focus remains on understanding and planning public engagement rather than implementation.
