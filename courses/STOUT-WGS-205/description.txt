CGER - Humanities & Arts Stout Core - Arts & Humanities Repeatable for Credit: Yes, up to 6 credits (but only if study away option is taken for one offering) An interdisciplinary introduction to LGBTQIA+ studies, including rhetoric and terminology; history; social and political movements; intersectionality; legal, medical, and human rights; ethical and moral debates; and cultural contributions (art, literature, music, etc.) through the lens of queer theory and from a global framework.
