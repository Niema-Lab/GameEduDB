This course introduces the practice of game development. Using the Unity engine, students will take a variety of thematic assignments from concept to finish, familiarizing themselves with its core elements: programming, asset creation and project management. Students will work independently and/or in pairs and groups. Emphasis is on process and proficiency using video game development tools. Prerequisites: None Fulfills: Video Game Elective (GTP students) or Digital Media Elective (Art Education students) or Studio Elective
