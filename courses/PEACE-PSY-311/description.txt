Prerequisite: PSY 101 In Cognition and Neuroscience, we are exploring how we think and the different processes we use, while also considering the interactions between brain and behavior. In part 1 of this course, we will explore topics such as neurons, the brain, cognitive development and learning, memory (including memory errors), intelligence, sleep and consciousness, and dreams. Information from this course can be applied to the areas of education, sport, business, law, and medicine.
