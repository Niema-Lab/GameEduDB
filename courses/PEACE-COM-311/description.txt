Prerequisite: COM 230 or CC 101 & COM 200 Digital media dominate the global communication landscape, making skills in writing for websites, social media and other online formats crucial to any organization. Students build these skills by creating their own professional blogs and promoting them through social media, producing social media content on behalf of other clients, and using analytical tools to measure and engage audiences. In the process, they’ll consider the many ethical and legal issues that underpin communication in the digital age. Offered fall.
