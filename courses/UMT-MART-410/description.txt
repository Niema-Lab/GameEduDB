This course explores how to formally analyze games and interactive media. Students engage in a variety of analytic writing styles within game studies, from journalistic reviews to feature reports and interpretative analyses, and construct written arguments for both academic and professional audiences. Through close reading and critical play, analytic writing, and iterative revision, students develop a methodological toolkit they can employ to conduct formal analyses of games and interactive media. Emphasizing games as complex and interactive texts, this course covers topics such as narrative design, mechanics, spatial storytelling, interface, and intertextuality. By the end of the semester, students will have a critical vocabulary they can employ when writing and talking about games, insight into how analytical frameworks can inform their own game design work, and gain a deeper understanding of games as a form of interactive art. Level: Undergraduate
