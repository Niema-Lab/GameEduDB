How are artists and designers thinking and making in the 21st Century? What are similarities and differences in how creators research, experiment, and solve problems? How does history, geography, and culture affect the multitude of ways works of art and design are made and experienced? Contemporary Frameworks is a Foundation studio course that explores these ideas through an inquiry-based, experiential approach to making. Students will investigate creative approaches used by contemporary artists and designers while exploring their own points of view in relation to those approaches Prerequisites: None Fulfills: Foundation Studio Core Requirement
