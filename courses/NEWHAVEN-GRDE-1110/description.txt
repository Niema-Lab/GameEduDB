This is a basic introductory course on how to use the Macintosh and common computer graphics software Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign). Students will explore creativity while strengthening and building their communication skills visually and verbally through critiques, discussions, and projects. This is a foundation course for any student who plans to enter the fields of graphic design, digital illustration/imaging or art. Laboratory fee; 3 credits.
