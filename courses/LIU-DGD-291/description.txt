The entire Digital Game Design and Development program leads to the Senior Game Project. This class requires students to bring together everything they have learned to create a major final project. The Senior Game Project allows students to work as individuals or in groups of any size. The primary requirement is that, by the end of the semester, each student has created a complete, polished, and successful game. A pre requisite of DGD 201, DGD 204, DGD 205 and DGD 206 is required.
