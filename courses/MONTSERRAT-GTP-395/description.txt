This course provides an in-depth study of a topic in Games Toy Play. The topic may be selected to take advantage of special events, to allow further exploration of a subject covered in a preliminary way in other courses, or to explore areas not sufficiently covered by the regular class rotation. Prerequisites: To be developed in conjunction with the course description for each topic Fulfills: 300-Level Studio Elective or Studio Elective or other Elective options as determined by faculty approval
