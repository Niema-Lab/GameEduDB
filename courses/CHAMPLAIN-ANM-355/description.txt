Building directly on Rigging Fundamentals, students develop a complete, production-ready character rig from the ground up. The course covers advanced joint chains, space-switching, corrective blend shapes, set driven key facial controls, deformer optimization, and rig documentation practices used in professional studios. Students are expected to build rigs that are clean, efficient, and actually usable by someone else. The course culminates in a fully documented character rig suitable for an animation or VFX production portfolio.
