The students in this course will continue working on the projects that were begun in Senior Studio 1. Throughout the term, students will continue the iterative work of developing their capstone project through a flexible but structured process in which they push from their previous proof of concept to a more polished version of a playable vertical slice or demo. The students will be expected to plan and document their development process from beginning to end. Projects will be continually refined through regular feedback sessions with instructors, mentors, and peers. This project will play a large role in showcasing what the students can add to a professional development team after graduation.
