Art History and Visual Culture Studies are disciplines that examine works of art and design in their historical and social contexts. This course introduces students to the foundational questions and practices that are the primary focus of art history and visual culture studies. Drawing from the history of the College’s major disciplines, students will explore the significance of art and design through an examination of their social roles, foundations in aesthetics, and importance as historical documents or artifacts as a way of interpreting reality. Students will develop historical literacy, learn key terms and concepts, review methods of analysis, and practice techniques used to analyze visual images, objects, and spaces. At the conclusion of the course students will demonstrate critical seeing, reading, and thinking by analyzing art, media, design, and text through writing and discussion.
