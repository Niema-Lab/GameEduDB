This course introduces students to the various forms and workflows in the creation of art and assets in two-dimensional video games. We will look at the various styles and aspects of 2D games such as pixel art, side-scrollers, top down, isometric, character design, environment design, concept art, animation, and user interfaces. This course is project based and centers around developing the students' own creative concepts and portfolios. This course acts as a foundation for the various pathways in game art.
