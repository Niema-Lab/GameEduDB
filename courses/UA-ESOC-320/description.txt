This course is a comprehensive exploration of artificial intelligence (AI) tools and approaches across various contexts and disciplines such as information science, data science, game design, and digital media. Students will develop competencies and skills in using AI technologies for finding information, data analysis, content creation, and prompt engineering, as well as fostering a core set of ethical standards for AI usage. Through practical application and critical examination, students will assess the broader implications of AI on professional practices and societal interactions. This course equips students to effectively implement and innovate with AI in diverse professional environments, ensuring they understand both the technological aspects and the transformative impact of AI on global and cultural scales.
