Bringing to life three-dimensional objects. Students will study and practice the integration of 3D animation production methods and techniques used in today's video game industry. Studies include the integration of motion- capture data and traditional key-frame animation into game engine production pipelines. Students will demonstrate how forward and inverse kinematics systems relate to body mechanics in order to effectively express a complex 3D animation network. Studio. Prerequisite: GAME 238, Character Design and Modeling and GAME 308, 3D Cinematic Animation.
