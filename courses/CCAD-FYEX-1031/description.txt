This course assists new students in making a successful transition to CCAD, both academically and personally by examining the ways they can work individually and collaboratively in creative environments. Fostering a sense of belonging, promoting engagement in curricular and co-curricular life, encouraging self-responsibility, building community agreements, and articulating to students the expectations and values of the College are half the components of the course. The other half focuses on creative problem identification, interrogative design, and an introduction to art and design processes.
