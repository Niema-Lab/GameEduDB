Game Media production 2D prepares students from any discipline to create game-ready digital and physical assets. Over the course of the semester students will be tasked with customizing an existing 2D digital game template with original content as well as printed components for tabletop. Topics such as optimization for 2D art assets in games, user interfaces, visual fx, 2D animation, multi-device compatibility, modular asset design, 2D stage design and feedback loops will be covered in-depth. Students are required to document their process throughout the semester online through an official game development platform.
