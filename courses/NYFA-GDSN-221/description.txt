This course exposes students to the intermediate technology and the increased complexity of 3D game development. Each student gets the experience of running their own game studio in collaboration with 1-4 classmates. Students deliver a working digital game at the end of the semester. Industry standards such as Agile, Scrum, Confluence and JIRA expose students to state of the art production methods and enable teams to deliver software efficiently. At the end of the degree students will have a portfolio of working software projects.
