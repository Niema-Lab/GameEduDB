In this course, Fine Art, Art History, and Games & Animation undergraduate students will engage in self-exploration, reflection, and goal alignment artistic and career journeys; build a suite of professional tools (professional portfolio website or comprehensive professional career map, resume, cover letter, LinkedIn); develop relevant strategies and skills for effective participation in the employment process; and acquaint students with employer performance expectations and workplace culture. This course will further develop students' sense of purpose and professionalism in the arts by connecting the skills developed through the study of their discipline with corresponding career interests. Students will analyze and discuss the social and ethical dimensions of the professional workplace, and develop an understanding of how professional development contributes to appropriate social and ethical behaviors in these constructs. By examining the career paths and personal journeys of active professionals working in or with creative disciplines, students will increase their understanding of what is often a non-linear path for artists and creative professionals, and begin applying this to their own professional development process. - Prerequisite Definition: To take this course you must: Be enrolled in one of these Plans GAA-BFA, ARTH-BAARH, FAA-BFA.
