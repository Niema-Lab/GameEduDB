The first in a two-course sequence of iterative game production classes, which build in complexity and skill in preparation for the Senior Game Project in digital, analog, and mixed genres. Junior Game Project 1 tasks students to complete individual and collaborative game projects in analog, digital, and/or mixed genres, practicing and developing their technical skills, practicing and refining their capacities within the games development process, and developing their artistic voice. Under the supervision of faculty, students will work individually and in collaboration with peers on an original game development project.
