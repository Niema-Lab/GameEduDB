This course comprehensively introduces digital design and media production principles and techniques. Students will gain proficiency in using industry-standard digital image creation and editing, vector graphics, and audio editing software. In addition to technical software skills, the course will cover the underlying theories and principles of visual and audio design. Topics will include color theory, typography, composition, and sound design. Students will learn to apply these conceptual foundations to create visually compelling and sonically engaging digital content. Through hands-on projects, students will practice integrating text, images, graphics, and audio to produce professional-quality media assets. The course emphasizes a creative problem-solving approach, allowing students to develop their artistic and technical abilities to communicate ideas and stories effectively in the digital realm. By the end of the course, students will have a deeper understanding of the fundamental principles underlying effective visual and audio design. This knowledge and practical experience will prepare students for further study or careers in graphic design, digital marketing, web development, game and software development, video production, and various creative industries.
