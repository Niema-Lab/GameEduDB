Prerequisites: COM 270 or SGD 111 or Permission of instructor Learn the artistic and technical processes of making music for the screen including film and video games. No prior music experience or knowledge needed! Explore how genre and visuals impact musical approaches while being immersed in the training process of making music through hands-on projects. Course topics will include music composition, music studio production, music theory fundamentals, soundscapes, and music technology (including digital/audio sound).
