This course will introduce students to the basics of inclusive game design from a sociological perspective, as well as game analysis and media literacy. Topics covered include: race, gender, disability, queer games, using games to foster social change, and the gaming industry. Students will draw on sociological methods to analyze existing games (both analog and digital) and learn applied methods for fostering social inclusion through game design. Students will further be exposed to game and media literacy, critical thinking and analysis from a social science perspective. The course will also engage with the sociological perspectives on power, privilege, and representation, and how to engage with them in the context of games.
