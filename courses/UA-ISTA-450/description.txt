This course provides a broad technical introduction to the methods and concepts of artificial intelligence. This course will focus on artificial intelligence as a set of tools for solving problems and for expanding our knowledge through inference. In order to apply these tools effectively, students will develop (i) a firm understanding of important AI algorithms and (ii) the ability to analyze real-world problems and apply an appropriate algorithm. Students will also gain practical experience writing programs that employ these algorithms. Specific course topics will include: Search (uninformed and informed), adversarial games, constraint satisfaction problems, knowledge representation, logical inference, probabilistic inference, and reinforcement learning.
