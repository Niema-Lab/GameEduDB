This course invites students to explore how artificial intelligence can expand creative thinking, problem-solving, and communication across fields. Designed for learners who already have a basic understanding of AI concepts, the course focuses on applying generative tools to real-world projects in areas such as design, media, writing, and data visualization. Students will learn how to plan, create, and evaluate AI-generated content using accessible, evolving technologies rather than focusing on any one platform. Emphasis is placed on experimentation, collaboration, and ethical decision-making as students develop the confidence to integrate AI into their personal, academic, and professional work.
