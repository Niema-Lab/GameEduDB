Provides historical context for the music of our age, including study of the Western European musical canon, American popular music, and related global musical traditions including those from Africa, Asia, and their respective diasporas. Students develop listening skills and understand the roots, influences and cross-cultural impacts of music in their own time and place through experience with music listening, readings, discussion and comparative analysis. Particular attention will be given to students finding disparate global roots for newly emerging musical movements. This course fulfills the requirement for FRM-128 Global Contexts in the Creative Arts, Media and Humanities.
