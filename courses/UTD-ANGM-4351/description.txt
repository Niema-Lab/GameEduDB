In the Immersive Technologies class, students will embark on an exciting journey into the world of storytelling through augmented and virtual reality technologies. This hands-on course is designed to provide students with practical experience in developing virtual reality (VR) and augmented reality (AR) applications and games for real world clients. May be repeated for credit as projects vary (6 semester credit hours maximum). Prerequisite: ANGM 3380 or ANGM 3381.
