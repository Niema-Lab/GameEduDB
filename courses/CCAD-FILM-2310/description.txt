Explores the theoretical and practical principles of lighting for film and video production. Students develop an understanding of the shared vocabulary, techniques, and workflows used to shape light for visual storytelling. Emphasis is placed on the conceptualization, planning, and execution of lighting designs that support narrative intent, visual composition, and emotional resonance. Through hands-on projects, students learn to design and employ lighting strategies for a range of production environments, developing the technical and creative skills necessary for cinematography and film/video production.
