This hands-on course exposes students to the advanced responsibility of choosing their own technology (in collaboration with teammates) for their BFA thesis project. Each student gets the experience of running their own game studio in collaboration with 1-4 classmates. Students work to create a functional digital game. Industry standards such as Agile, Scrum, Confluence and JIRA expose students to state of the art production methods and enable teams to deliver software efficiently. Students are encouraged to continue and polish the project they began in the Collaborative Thesis I. At the end of the degree students will have a portfolio of working software projects.
