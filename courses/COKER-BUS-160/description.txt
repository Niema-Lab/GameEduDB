An introduction to applications used in the business industry. The focus of this course is on business productivity software applications and professional behavior in computing, including word processing, spreadsheets, databases, presentation graphics, and business-oriented utilization of the Internet. Through a series of hands-on activities, students will create, analyze, and critique reports, letters, project plans, presentations, spreadsheets, and other professional communications. Student's are required to have or use a device which supports the Microsoft Office Suite of products used in the class.
