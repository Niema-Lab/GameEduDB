Examines how technology is used by listeners, creators, critics, remixers and a wide variety of other roles to participate in and gain a deeper understanding of music and its role in our lives. Covers the fundamentals and themes of working with digital sound. Introduces examples of practical ways to work with digital sound through lecture, discussion and hands-on activities. Covers key historical and theoretical concepts of digital sound and electroacoustic music. Allows students to develop sonic and musical skills by working directly with various software programs as well as basic digital signal processing and editing techniques. Introduces students to creative coding languages as well as digital audio workstations (DAWs).
