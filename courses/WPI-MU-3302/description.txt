This course examines topics in Music Technology in which Digital Sound plays a significant role. Topics may vary each year among the following areas: digital editing, audio recording, film scoring, game audio, sound effects, audio production, theatrical sound, and surround sound. Students can take MU 3302 only one time for credit, but a student interested in taking another version can take a second one as an ISU. Students that have earned credit for MU 3615 cannot receive credit for MU 3302.
