Brand Manager, Marketing Researcher, Promotions Director, Digital Marketing Specialist, these are just a few titles of the many jobs available in marketing today. In this course, we are going on a semester-long journey to explore the field of marketing, as well as to learn about what marketing professionals do each day. Together, we will examine the environmental forces impacting the marketing of products and services. With the customer at the center of all of our discussions, we’ll focus on marketing’s role in developing, pricing, promoting, and distributing viable products, services, and even ideas. You will have the opportunity to apply your marketing skills when you work in teams to create a marketing plan.
