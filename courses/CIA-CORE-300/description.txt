CORE 300 advances the process-based framework of inquiry, iteration, and exploration into more independent, self-directed projects. Through topic-based studios, students synthesize analog and digital methods while applying creative processes with increasing agility and intention. Emphasis is placed on conceptual depth, critical reflection, and the ability to articulate ideas across media and contexts. Students cultivate the independence and integrative approaches needed for advanced studio work and for defining the direction of their emerging practice. Prerequisites: CORE 100 and CORE 200. 3 credits.
