Digital Animation I is an introduction to motion graphics and animation using Adobe Photoshop and After Effects. The class will provide students with a working knowledge of both programs, as well as practical ways to use them to create motion graphics, animated video, UX, / UI, and web design projects. The first part of the course will cover creating dynamic text and graphics in Photoshop, and the second half will cover animating those graphics, typography and image in After Effects.
