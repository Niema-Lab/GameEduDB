What elements do game designers consider when they create a video game? Students will tour the big picture of video game design: players, rules, gameplay, writing and narrative design, goals, and the underlying coding and game architecture that makes it all happen. Students sample games from the past and present to identify what makes an engaging digital game. No programming is required. An overview of the game development process and exploration of some indie tools such as Blender may be included in a given semester.
