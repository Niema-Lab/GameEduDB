Prerequisite: DGAD 2001 or GRDE 1110 or permission of instructor. This course is an introduction to 3D modeling and animation. Students will explore concepts associated with three-dimensional space and use of the software to render 3D forms, structures, and virtual environments. The course will also cover the principles of animation and time-based media editing. Critical issues associated with computer-generated imagery will also be addressed. Laboratory fee. 3 credits.
