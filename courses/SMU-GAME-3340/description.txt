Explores the use of generative AI technologies to modify and iterate on game content. Students learn how to leverage AI tools for procedural art, narrative generation, and assisted code while understanding the technical, creative, and ethical considerations involved. Through hands-on projects and experiments, students integrate AI-driven content into playable prototypes, evaluate its impact on gameplay and player experience, and explore workflows that combine human creativity with AI augmentation. Prerequisite: DSIN 2350.
