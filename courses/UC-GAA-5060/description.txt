In this practice-based course students create an ambitious project in games and/or animation. Students may manage large-scale creation efforts among a broader group of students, in their second through fourth year of study, or choose to work on an auteur project of sufficient depth and complexity. The outcome of this course will be an exhibition and/or festival-worthy final product. - Prerequisite Definition: To take this course you must: Be enrolled in the following Plan GAA-BFA. Be at least a Senior.
