In this course, students learn to navigate campus resources, build professional relationships with faculty, staff, and peers, develop effective time management and study skills, and work collaboratively in a college environment. Workshops covering information literacy and financial management, institutional expectations, academic advising, self-advocacy, and health and wellness resources will help students build pathways to success in their transition to college. Through meaningful campus engagement and community-building experiences, students will also cultivate a sense of belonging, develop confidence in their abilities, and establish connections that support their academic, personal, and creative success.
