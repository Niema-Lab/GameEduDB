CORE 200 extends the process-based approach to art and design introduced in the first year. Through topic-based studios, students pursue more complex questions, applying and adapting creative processes across analog and digital methods. Emphasis is placed on refining strategies, sustaining inquiry over time, and integrating reflection and documentation into an evolving practice. Students deepen their conceptual awareness and begin to connect multiple perspectives, preparing them to navigate more advanced and interdisciplinary approaches in their creative work. Prerequisite: CORE 100. 3 credits.
