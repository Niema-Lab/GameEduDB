This course focuses on the concerns of digital game design, including UI, level design, player dynamics and game flow. These aspects are addressed through thematic projects, where students create and playtest many potential solutions. Students will work independently and/or in pairs and groups. Emphasis is on creative problem solving and innovation. Prerequisites: GTP260 Intro to Video Game Development or GD200 Graphic Design I or GD212 Typography I or Permission of Instructor Fulfills: 300-Level Studio Elective or Project Design Elective (Interdisciplinary Arts students) or Studio Elective
