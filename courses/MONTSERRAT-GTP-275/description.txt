This course focuses on the practice of 3D video game development. Through thematic projects, students will practice concepts unique to 3D video games, work on 3D concerns found in all games and integrate 3D tools with their game engine. Students will work independently and/or in pairs and groups. Emphasis is on making creative works. Prerequisites: None Fulfills: Video Game Elective (GTP students) or Digital Media Elective (Art Education students) or Studio Elective
