Prerequisites: ART 3143 and ART 3153. This studio provides gamers and animators an in depth opportunity to explore their visual voice whether it is user interface relationships in the 3D or 2D, or strictly exploring avenues in character, story or design development. A broad variety of technologies using visual applications requiring advanced knowledge of computer technology applications, programs and languages, and requiring a deep understanding of the visual arts and the elements of design is necessary. 3 hours. Spring.
