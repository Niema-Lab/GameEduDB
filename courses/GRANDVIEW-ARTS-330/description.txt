By examining a variety of texts - writings, films, paintings, new media, and performative works - students will become better acquainted with the phenomenon of global visual culture. Theories of rhetoric will be applied in analyzing cultural artifacts, their creation and impact on societies. Of particular interest is a comparison of the marketing of ideas and products in various cultures. Core outcomes met: IC, W. Prerequisite Completion of university composition requirement with a C minus (C-) or above. Offered Spring.
