This course builds upon the foundations established in the Introduction to Game Design course, and focuses on advanced study of system design and play mechanics. The course is workshop-focused, meaning a substantial portion of time is spent actively engaged in the paper prototyping process. Students explore more sophisticated facets of the playable systems and user experience design. Students spend time learning more difficult subjects like game balancing and game economics. Creating system literacy is the primary goal; and everything else we do supports that aim.
