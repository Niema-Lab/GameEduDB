Study of the fundamental concepts and the necessary criteria to be able to make appropriate decisions in the design processes of a video game. Discussion of the local and global impact of videogames on individuals, organizations, and society. Evaluation of the artistic, mechanical, logistical, and commercial aspects of a video game. Design of a video game from its conceptual stage to its concretization in a design document. Drafting a video game design document. Requires 30 hours of conference and 30 hours of closed laboratory.
