This course focuses on the cycle of playtesting and iteration: making a prototype, playtesting, revising the design, and trying once again. Students will practice structuring not only their game projects, but also a coherent process of assembly and soliciting feedback. Students will acquire a working knowledge of recent experimental games, choose a games medium among them, and create their own product. They will combine a games-critical analytic framework with a praxis-based process of iterative design.
