This course focuses heavily on production and gives students the experience of completing an entire game production cycle from concept to implementation to polish to launch. This course runs one semester, but is accompanied by two studio classes over the course of two semesters. Each student gets the experience of running their own game studio in collaboration with 1-4 classmates. Students deliver a working digital game at the end of the semester. Industry standards such as Agile, Scrum, Confluence and JIRA expose students to state of the art production methods and enable teams to deliver software efficiently. Students take increasingly advanced variations of this course in the course of their degree so they will acquire more sophisticated skills each successive semester. At the end of the degree students will have a portfolio of working software projects.
