This course delves into the horror genre across multiple mediums, including television, film, video and tabletop games, as well as location-based experiences. Students will explore the cultural significance, historical evolution, and enduring appeal of horror, examining how the genre has adapted to different formats and technological advancements. Through analysis of iconic works and hands-on projects, the class will investigate key themes such as fear, suspense, and the psychology of horror, as well as the societal and cultural factors that influence the genre.
