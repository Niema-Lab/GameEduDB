Principles and elements of motion design through application and studio practices; the relationship between typography principles and animation fundamentals; advanced applied approach to the language and principles of motion-building, synthesizing the language of motion with narrative and narrative structure. Examination of systems, structure, and syn- thesis to text and image for time-based media; knowledge and skills in digital creativity emphasized throughout. Four contact hours per week. Prerequisites: ART 2523, 3623, 3523. (TBA)
