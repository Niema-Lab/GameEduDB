Few experiences are as rewarding for a game developer as a game jam. The goal of these game jams is to create the best game possible within a given theme and time limit. This course takes these core elements but focuses specifically on the artistic side of the game jam experience. Students will be presented with a diverse set of aesthetic themes, constraints, and objectives that will challenge their creative abilities. They will produce a wide range of game art assets varying in style, purpose, and type in both individual and team settings. This course is ideal for students who wish to diversify their game art portfolio while utilizing a wide range of artistic talents, knowledge, and software. Due to the changing nature of the challenges, this course can be taken twice and credits received both times toward the appropriate degree.
