This class will examine the creation and application of music and sound design as used in contemporary games, video, and other digital media. A focus will be placed on the relationship of audio to user experience and as a method of feedback in traditional and non-traditional gaming systems. The student will learn how to mix and synchronize soundtrack elements to video. Both the development process and consideration of final product will be explored. Enrollment in DIG250 includes a $60 course fee which covers the purchase of Avid’s Pro Tools 130: Pro Tools for Game Audio eBook, as well as two attempts to complete the PT130 certification exam.
