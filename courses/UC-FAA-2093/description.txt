In this studio course, students will carry out intensive study in the creation of original digital 3d forms using reference material, that can be applied to game design, animation, and/or sculptural output; and will study the work of professional artists working in the field. Course activities will include lectures, demos, student presentations, studio work, field trips and critiques. - Prerequisite Definition: To take this course you must: Be enrolled in one of these Plans DMC-BIS, FAA-BFA, FAA-MIN.
