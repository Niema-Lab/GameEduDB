This course is designed to provide students flexibility while participating in a credit-bearing internship experience. In-person and online oversight of the experience will be provided by Career and Professional Development staff throughout the duration of the internship experience, providing students flexibility while exploring career opportunities, obtaining meaningful exposure to a college-level career field, and developing new skills under the direction of industry professionals. To participate in the course, an approved internship must be provided by an off-campus company or organization, spanning at least six weeks during an academic term. Rather than providing a letter grade at the conclusion of the CPAM Internship class, CR will be entered on the student’s transcript indicating successful completion of the internship course. Failure to successfully complete the course will result in a grade of N (no pass). Successful completion of the course is contingent upon satisfying the minimum number of required work hours at the internship site as well as submission of additional written assignments. The CPAM Internship class may be taken for one, two, or three credit hours, corresponding to the number of hours worked at the internship site.
