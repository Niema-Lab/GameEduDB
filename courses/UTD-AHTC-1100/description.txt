Graduation requirement for all freshmen in the School of Arts, Humanities, and Technology (AHT). Topics include: intellectual and cultural environment in AHT through lectures, activities, guest panels, A&H majors, research opportunities, careers, and internships. Open to undeclared majors interested in AHT. Required for all first-time in college freshman students in their first semester majoring in the Harry W. Bass Jr. School of Arts, Humanities, and Technology. Credit cannot be received for both courses, AHTC 1100 and AHTC 2100.
