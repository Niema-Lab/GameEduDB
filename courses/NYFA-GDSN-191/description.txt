This course exposes students to the technology and commercial design aspects of mobile game development. Students will be exposed to a variety of standard mobile game technologies (eg: GPS, accelerometers, augmented reality), design concepts (including a variety of Free-to-Play mechanics), and terminology (eg: KPI, ARPDAU, Gacha, hypercasual). For the project they create, students will have to take into consideration the varying screen resolutions and control schemes of any mobile devices they target. Students will also need to identify and design around an appropriately identified monetization strategy. Each student gets the experience of running their own game studio in collaboration with 1-4 classmates. Students deliver a working digital game at the end of the semester. Industry standards such as Agile, Scrum, Confluence and JIRA expose students to state of the art production methods and enable teams to deliver software efficiently.
