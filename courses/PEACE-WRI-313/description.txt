Prerequisite: Successful completion of a Global Citizenship course This course examines the fundamental elements of classic narrative, such as plot, character, and point of view, and asks students to apply the logic of their traditional use to the quickly evolving world of video games. Is Halo’s Master Chief a character or an empty type? How much of the kingdom of Hyrule do you need to explore to understand the plot of Breath of the Wild? In this class, we look at stories, movies, and other artifacts of popular culture and ask whether video games are rewriting our sense of story or just applying old patterns to new consoles.
