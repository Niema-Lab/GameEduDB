This course introduces the conceptual process of environment design for the entertainment industry, focusing on creating unique and compelling interiors, exteriors, props, and vehicles across a variety of stories and historical eras. Students will examine the aesthetic, cultural, and historical context of architecture, the behavior of organic forms, and the principles of design and construction to visually communicate ideas and narrative elements effectively. Through silhouettes, thumbnail sketches, and renderings, students will develop original design solutions that support a cohesive visual narrative. Emphasis is placed on understanding how things are built, integrating industrial design principles, and mastering illustration skills. Students will gain a solid foundation in creating environments using traditional methods, available technology, and 3D visualization techniques
