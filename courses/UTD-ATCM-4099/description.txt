Intended for students pursuing major honors with a thesis in addition to advanced coursework. Students will write a paper connecting their concentration's central questions to the eligible organized course in which they are enrolled. Restricted to students majoring in Arts, Technology, and Emerging Communication who are within one semester of graduation and who meet honors requirements. Credit/No Credit only. Department consent required. Corequisite: ATCM 4332 or ATCM 4333 or ATCM 4334.
