Game Pipeline Capstone I prepares students for their final year developing a proposed game experience. Collaboration is strongly encouraged. Students will prepare original content from early prototyping to functional game-play. Core production and concept development are covered. Students will develop a supplemental art-bible, design document, development updates through official online platforms and produce professional level art assets of their choice, be it 3D, 2D or experimental. Through faculty and peer mentorship, students will produce an industry standard demo-reel documenting game-play and updated digital portfolio including a playable demo.
