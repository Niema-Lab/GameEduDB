Have you ever wondered what makes games so “addicting”? Game designers understand their target audiences: Who will be attracted to a game, which players will keep coming back for more, and how will players behave. Students identify and analyze the different features and feedback loops that encourage people to play digital games. Drawing on insights from psychology as well as business, students explore the ethical and financial implications of living in a digital world in which attention and engagement are measured, valued, and commodified.
