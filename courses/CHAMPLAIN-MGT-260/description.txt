Students will work in teams to define project scope, specifications, and assumptions while developing essential skills for coordinating people, processes, and resources. Students will explore project management methodologies including Waterfall, Scrum, Kanban, Lean, and hybrid approaches. Students will engage with stakeholders, develop work breakdown structures and Gantt charts, and use project management software to manage scheduling and facilitate team communication. Students will plan and execute projects for campus or community clients and deliver documentation and results that meet stakeholder expectations.
