Game Jams presents a hands-on environment for multi-disciplinary collaboration where the focus is producing a game and/or interactive experience. Community partners, and departments across campus are invited to work alongside students. Students will work in teams to produce final experiences meeting industry quality standards. Being an exploratory course, students are encouraged to experiment and propose unique solutions for the present design challenges. Students will have expert guidance and mentoring opportunities working alongside external stakeholders and faculty. Alongside production tasks students will master their skills in meeting etiquette, public speaking, team-building, business models for creative tech, project and task management.
