This course is the first stage of the senior/capstone experience of the Game Design, Game Programming, and Game Art Majors. It represents the pre-production stage of the capstone project and is required for all students majoring in Game. Students are exposed to best team practices, overall project management, art, sound, programming, and design pre-production techniques and requirements. They also participate in finalizing plans for launching their career in the context of the team. The demo games will be presented in a professional public context.
