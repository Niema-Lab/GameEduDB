This course will build upon the techniques and principles introduced in the Story & Screen III course with emphasis on the development of concepts and material for senior capstone projects. Idea generation, shot lists, storyboards, production schedules, and scripts will be completed and organized into cohesive senior project proposal packages. Visual assets including concept art of characters, environments, and props will be developed and included in the proposal package. Two packages will be developed and pitched to faculty during the semester, with at least one “greenlit” to move into production during Senior Studio I and II. Prerequisite: AGA 311 Story & Screen III
