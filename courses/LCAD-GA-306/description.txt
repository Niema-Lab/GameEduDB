This course explores the diverse genres of digital games, providing students with an understanding of popular genres such as first-person shooters, platformers, and multiplayer online games. Through in-depth analysis, students will examine the key elements that define each genre, including mechanics, player challenges, emotional engagement, and reward systems. Emphasis will be placed on understanding how these components influence player experience and contribute to a game’s overall success. By the end of the course, students will develop insights into what makes different genres effective and how these insights apply to game design.
