Prerequisites: VGD 1002 and CUS 1100. This is the second half of the Capstone sequence in Game Development & Emerging Media, focusing on the production and packaging of projects proposed in VGD4001 Capstone I. Students extend and refine working proofs-of-concept toward the completion of a major project. In addition to production and critique, students professionalize their craft, creating a press kit, distribution and community engagement plan, and refining their digital portfolio.
