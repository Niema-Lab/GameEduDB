This advanced technical art course focuses on the creation and integration of digital costumes for interactive 3D characters. Students explore the design, construction, and optimization of virtual garments using procedural modeling pipelines and industry-standard digital fashion tools. Emphasis is placed on translating artistic concepts into technically efficient assets that perform effectively within real-time game engines. Through a combination of design theory, simulation techniques, and practical implementation, students learn how to develop visually compelling and physically accurate costumes that enhance character identity and narrative immersion. The course also introduces advanced topics in deformation systems, rigging integration, and performance optimization for complex character assets. Students produce a final project demonstrating the full pipeline of digital costume design — from concept development to in-engine implementation — supported by research, critique, and iteration. Prerequisite: GAME 3354.
