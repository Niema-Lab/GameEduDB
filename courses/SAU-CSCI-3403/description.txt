Prerequisites: CSCI 3103. This course introduces artificial intelligence (AI) as a field of study and practice within computer science. Students explore how machines can perceive, reason, learn, and make decisions. Topics include search and optimization, machine learning, pattern recognition, natural language processing, and responsible use of AI technologies. Through programming assignments and projects, students apply AI techniques to practical problems drawn from business, healthcare, and everyday computing applications. 3 hours. As needed.
