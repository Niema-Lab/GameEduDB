What will your life look like after you graduate? What if you could create a clear path for your first five years post- graduation, sustaining your life’s needs through meaningful work, trying new things, and also pursuing your greatest passions? In this Career Design Studio course, you will apply the same creative processes you use in your studio classes to the life-changing work of designing your career. In Designing Your Career, students will research multiple career paths and devise five year plans that acknowledge both their lifelong passions and their practical needs for a financially stable life after graduation. After refining application materials in order to create multiple resume and portfolio packages that are focused on applying for specific types of work, they will participate in mock interviews and portfolio reviews with industry professionals, network within the fields that interest them, and apply for jobs or postgraduate education or internship options. Students will also participate in workshops that will prepare them for life after Montserrat on topics such as: apartment searches, taxes, and budgeting. To be completed during senior year, either during Fall Semester, Spring Semester, or Winter Session. Prerequisites: CDS200 Designing Your Montserrat Fulfills: Career Design Studio Requirement
