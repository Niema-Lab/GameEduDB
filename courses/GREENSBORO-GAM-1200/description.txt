Humans love games. Ancient peoples found ways to build some play into their daily life. Why do humans spend time and money on games? What do we look for when playing video games? Students explore key features of video games, with some attention to tabletop games. Students explore evidence of animal play, virtual/embodied play, flow, win state, game rules, competitive vs. cooperative experiences, single and multiplayer games, and identifying “fun factors”. This is a hands-on course that requires active exploration and play as part of the learning process.
