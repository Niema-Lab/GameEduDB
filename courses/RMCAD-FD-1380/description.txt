This freshman-level course is a continuation of Life Drawing I, with the inclusion of more concept-oriented issues in drawing the human figure. Students will experiment with matters of expression and further their skills in analyzing forms of the nude body. Emphasis on proportions, muscular structure, foreshortening, value, and color leads to comprehension of the human form. Upon successful completion of the course, students will have extensive practice in individualizing compositional strategies with the human figure.
