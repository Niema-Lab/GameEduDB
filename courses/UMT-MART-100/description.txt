Class meets on Mountain Campus in the U.C. Game Room. Explore the basics of various esports including rules, concepts, strategies, and mechanics. Semester concludes with a gaming tournament. Students must bring their own headset for team play. Graded Credit/No Credit based on participation and attendance policy. Students may include up to, but not more than, 4 credits earned in MART 100 and all other ACT 100-179 activity courses in the total number of credits required for graduation.
