This course investigates a fundamental understanding of 3D form and space through material exploration, design, and fabrication. Creative problem-solving strategies, including research, idea generation, drawings, and models, are used to develop three-dimensional forms. Students create and place three-dimensional forms in context to their surroundings, understanding the relationship to its environment. Students explore a variety of analog and digital techniques throughout the creative process that encourage innovative design solutions. This course may require the use of technical skills, physical exertion, and/or operation of sharp instruments or advanced equipment.
