This course introduces students to the work of writing creatively, encouraging them to become more attentive readers, as preparation for their own growth as writers. We’ll survey a range of literary texts, developing a deeper understanding of how creative genres work, what parameters structure them, and how they might be deployed in our own writing. Students will experiment within their own fiction, poetry, and nonfiction writing. The bedrock of our class will be peer review, so students can read and learn from each others’ work.
