This course examines topics in Music Technology in which the application of MIDI and MIDI systems play a significant role. Topics may vary each year among the following areas: sequencing, live performance, composition, and film scoring. Students can take MU 3301 only one time for credit, but a student interested in taking another version can take a second one as an ISU. Students that have earned credit for MU 3614 cannot receive credit for MU 3301.
