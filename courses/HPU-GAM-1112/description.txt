This course will teach some of the tools and technologies employed by in- dependent game design and development companies to de- sign, create, edit, store, and deliver content and assets to co- workers and end-users. Students create projects and submit to their digital portfolio. The featured tools taught in the class will change each semester, so it is repeatable for credit. Stud- ents should be careful not to repeat the same topic, and in- stead take a section that focuses on a different group of tools. Four credits.
