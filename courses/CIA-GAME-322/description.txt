The course is designed to teach students about the various elements of game development in 3D game engines. Students will work to utilize modern tools to develop 2D/3D graphical assets into an interactive 3D game engine through the use of programming. Students will learn how to prepare assets for use in Unity and other common game development software. The class will consist of weekly in-class lectures, demonstrations, and projects to be completed both in and out of class. A focus will be applied to skill learning while additional topics and theory will be covered to provide a well-rounded experience. 3 credits.
