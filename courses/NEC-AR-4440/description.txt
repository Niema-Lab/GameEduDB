This interdisciplinary capstone class allows students from the range of concentrations in the art department to address topic‐based projects from their own unique skill sets and perspectives. Group critiques and collaborative assignments will encourage students to broaden their perspectives on art and art making. Emphasis will also be placed on professional studio practice, skilled execution, and self‐direction. Prerequisite: 3000 level Fine Arts or Media Arts Course. Offered every fall. Credits: 4 May be repeated for credit. Required course for all art majors. Previous name: Fine and Media Arts Topics
