This course introduces students to cinematics design using industry standard software, including Unreal Engine, covering the complete pipeline from concept to final render. Students will learn environment set dressing, camera composition, animation and blocking in Sequencer, markerless motion capture for creating animations, and mocap data cleanup, character retargeting, audio design, and video editing, culminating in a fully rendered cinematic short. Students will learn the same real-time tools and processes used by professional game and film studios.
