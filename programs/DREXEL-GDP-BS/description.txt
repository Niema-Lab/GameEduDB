Drexel’s Game Design & Production (GDAP) BS program has earned a top-ten position for over two decades (on average across multiple national rankings, including the Princeton Review), and provides students with a broad foundation in digital design and content creation skills in team project environments. In Drexel’s generalist-to-specialist approach, students experience a wide variety of roles in bringing a game all the way through production – from pie-in-the-sky idea to playable reality. Through this process of discovery, students learn about themselves and the skill focuses that they want to pursue, minors and electives they want to study, and how to create a game as part of a team throughout the program.
