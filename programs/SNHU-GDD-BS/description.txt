The Game Design and Development program will immerse students in the creative and technical processes of making digital games, equipping them with the skills to design and develop functional and interactive experiences. Students will focus on specialties that include art, design, or programming while also gaining fundamental skills and knowledge in the areas of game mechanics, prototyping, and iteration. They will engage in collaborative projects that mirror industry workflows, preparing them to navigate the fast-evolving landscape of game development with professionalism and adaptability. Emphasizing communication, project management, and industry research considerations, the program will empower students to become innovative problem solvers and professionals in the game industry.
