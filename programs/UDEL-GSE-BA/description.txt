Students will demonstrate competence in using game design and development hardware and software, and in self-teaching to adapt to evolving platforms and applications. Students will demonstrate an understanding of the business practices and marketing strategies used by game publishers of varying sizes and budgets in order to promote their products, and to explain the pros and cons of these distinct approaches. Students will demonstrate an interdisciplinary understanding of how video games function as cultural products, from conception to completion and beyond, as they are continually referenced, recycled and reinvented by cultures around the world.
