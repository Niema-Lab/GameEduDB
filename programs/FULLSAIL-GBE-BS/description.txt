The Game Business and Esports Bachelor of Science at Full Sail University prepares students for careers in the gaming, esports, and interactive entertainment industries. Whether the goal is to work in esports management, game publishing, gaming marketing, business development, event production, partnerships, community management, or content strategy, this program is designed to help students build the skills employers seek. The gaming and esports industries rely on professionals who understand both gaming culture and the business strategies that drive successful products, brands, events, and communities. Throughout the program, students will explore how games are developed, marketed, distributed, monetized, and supported while gaining practical experience in the business operations that power the industry.
