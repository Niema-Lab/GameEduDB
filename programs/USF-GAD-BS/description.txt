The Game and Application Development program focuses on the creation of interactive software for entertainment and education. Game theory and development practices are also used to produce practical educational and industrial programs such as training and certification modules and phone apps in addition to conventional games. Game and Application Development students will be able to hone programming and artistic skills leveraging UFS’s strong reputation in creative arts. With a strong foundation in experiential learning and support from the liberal arts, the curriculum begins with a foundation in operating systems and programming, 2D and 3D composition, and animation. Students continue with the exploration of game theory, game development, user interfaces, and deployment. Upper-level courses focus on industrial gaming, modeling and multi-media law. Students completing the B.S. in Game and Application Development are prepared to be computer software designers and game developers, a profitable and rapidly growing field.
