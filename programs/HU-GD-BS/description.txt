Game Design students will gain an understanding of what it takes to be a professional game designer, including the capacity for students to articulate the industry's needs and constraints. Graduates of the program will be able to work in a diverse set of environments as professional game designers, capable of specifying and creating engaging and immersive gameplay experiences using industry-standard tools, processes and methods. Students will gain the hands-on skill to study, plan, design, and create digital games without needing to acquire full coding or game development skills.
