The Bachelor of Science in Game Development prepares undergraduate students with the technical development skills and design knowledge necessary to build virtual interactive environments across devices and platforms, focusing on the implementation and engineering of games for entertainment, as well as serious games and virtual reality simulations for training, education, healthcare, and other applications. The degree equips students with hands-on development experience and industry-relevant technical competencies, signaling to employers that graduates possess the programming fluency, systems thinking, and production skills essential for professional game development, while mastering the full development pipeline including technical architecture, engine implementation, optimization, and deployment, complemented by competency in design fundamentals, market analysis, and usability evaluation. The program serves students pursuing careers in the games industry by providing rigorous training in programming, software engineering, and computational problem-solving, alongside coursework in artistic and business aspects of game production, with students completing core courses in programming, algorithms, game engines, and systems design before choosing from advanced electives that allow specialization in areas such as graphics programming, network architecture, AI implementation, physics simulation, or entrepreneurial development practices. Throughout the curriculum, students apply software development methodologies and technical best practices to practical game projects, building a professional portfolio of completed games through both individual and team-based development, preparing graduates for technical roles in the digital games industry including gameplay programmer, engine programmer, tools developer, technical designer, and technical artist positions.
