A successful game artist and animator must be fluent in a wide range of tools and practices in a highly competitive industry. The BFA in Game Design and Animation is tailored toward aspiring digital artists who want to focus on the aesthetic experience of game design, digital art, and animation. With the revolution in software, students have new and powerful tools to help them tell stories, build worlds, create 2D or 3D animation, digital assets for game-based applications or multimedia, and visual and sound effects. Additionally, the curriculum identifies and cultivates the inevitable intersections between related disciplines to empower students with the critical and technical skills that produce sustainable art careers and industry professionals. The program promotes experiential learning to foster a diverse mixture of skills integrating artistic expression with technology.
